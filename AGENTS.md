# Outreach — project rules

Purpose: produce LinkedIn content (posts and outreach messages) in Ibrahim Elhofy's
voice. Persona and voice rules live in the بروفايل SOUL.md — this file is project facts only.

## Folders

- `CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md` — shared vocabulary and voice/format guidance for every
  writer agent. Read before writing; `tone/` samples still take priority.
- `tone/` — Ibrahim's own writing samples. Ground truth for his personal voice. Read
  before writing anything in his name.
- `references/` — viral posts by other people that he wants to write in the genre of
  (genre blueprints, not his words):
  - `references/experience/` — story→lesson posts
  - `references/news/` — new tool/news + practical implication + engagement question
  - `references/tutorial/` — concept explainers (clear, example-driven)
  - `references/wisdoms/` — distilled opinions/theories; more formal register
- `content/` — drafts and finished output go here.

## Workflow

1. Read `CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md`, then relevant samples from `tone/`.
2. Pick a genre from `references/` and study how the viral post is built.
3. Get the raw material for Ibrahim's own angle (story, news, concept, opinion).
4. Draft in Ibrahim's voice (see SOUL.md), using the genre's structure — not its words.
5. Ibrahim reviews; iterate until it sounds like him.

## Notes

- Source .md files are Arabic, UTF-8, CRLF. Save without a BOM.
- Voice authority, in order: tone/ samples > SOUL.md rules. If they disagree, the
  sample wins.
- Never copy phrasing from references/ — they belong to their authors. Take structure
  and pace only.
